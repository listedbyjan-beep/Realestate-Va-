# The Campaigns

Each campaign below is ready to brief a designer or to build in Ads Manager or Google Ads. Ad copy is written in a warm, confident, local voice. Every ad needs the brokerage name (see the compliance checklist).

---

## Campaign 1: "What's Your Home Worth?" (Seller Lead Gen) ⭐ Priority
**Goal:** Seller leads (the highest-value clients)
**Channels:** Meta lead form + Google Search
**Offer:** A free, personal home value report prepared by Jan, not an automated estimate
**Audience:** Meta Housing category, 15+ mile radius centered on your core area (e.g., central San Diego), broad targeting; Google keywords below

**Hooks to test**
1. "Zillow guessed. Jan *knows*." Get a real San Diego valuation in 24 hours.
2. Homes in `[NEIGHBORHOOD]` are still getting strong offers. Find out what yours could sell for today.
3. Thinking of selling in 2027? Start with your number, not a guess.
4. Bought before 2020? You may be sitting on more San Diego equity than you think.
5. Does your lot have ADU potential? That can change your home's value. I'll include it in your report.

**Primary text (Meta)**
> Online estimates can be off by tens of thousands of dollars. 🏡
> I'll prepare a free, no-pressure Home Value Report for your address, based on what's *actually* selling on your street.
> ✅ Recent comparable sales ✅ Upgrades that add value ✅ Suggested list price range
> Tap "Get My Report" and I'll have it to you within 24 hours.
> Jan `[LAST NAME]`, REALTOR® · `[BROKERAGE NAME]` · DRE #`[NUMBER]`

**Headline:** Your Free Home Value Report · **CTA:** Get Quote / Learn More
**Lead form questions:** Address, timeline (0–3 months / 3–6 / 6–12 / just curious), name, phone, email
**Google keywords:** "what is my home worth san diego", "sell my house san diego", "best realtor to sell my home in `[neighborhood]`" (e.g., North Park, Clairemont, Chula Vista, Carlsbad, Escondido), "home valuation san diego county"
**Google RSA headlines:** "Free San Diego Home Valuation" · "Sell Your Home for Top Dollar" · "Local Expert. Real Numbers." · "Report in 24 Hours"
**Creative:** 30-second selfie video of Jan in front of a sold sign, plus a carousel of "Sold in X days" cards
**Follow-up:** Call within 5 minutes → send the report within 24 hours → 6-month email/SMS nurture
**KPI:** Seller CPL ≤ $60; 15% of leads book a listing appointment

## Campaign 2: "Stop Renting Your Landlord's Dream" (First-Time Buyers)
**Goal:** Buyer leads and workshop registrations
**Channels:** Meta/Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
**Offer:** Free First-Time Buyer Guide (PDF) plus a monthly virtual workshop with a partner lender
**Hooks**
1. "You're paying `$[X]`/month in rent. Here's what that could buy in San Diego County."
2. "Down payment help most San Diego buyers don't know about." (e.g., CalHFA programs, the San Diego Housing Commission and County of San Diego first-time buyer programs. Check current availability, limits and funding before publishing.)
3. "Your credit score doesn't have to be perfect. Here's what lenders actually look at."
4. "Where first-time buyers can still get in: condos and townhomes in `[NEIGHBORHOOD]`."

**Primary text**
> Renting in San Diego? Your monthly rent might already cover a mortgage payment. 👀
> Join my free First-Time Buyer Workshop: how much you can afford, down payment help, and the step-by-step process, with no pressure.
> 📅 `[DATE]` · Virtual · Seats limited
> Jan `[LAST NAME]`, REALTOR® · `[BROKERAGE NAME]` · DRE #`[NUMBER]`

**Creative:** Rent-vs-own talking-head reels, myth-busting carousels, client "We got the keys!" stories (with written permission)
**KPI:** CPL ≤ $15; 30% workshop show rate; 10% become a buyer consult

## Campaign 3: "Moving to San Diego?" (Military & Out-of-State Relocation)
**Goal:** Out-of-area buyer leads
**Channels:** Google Search (keywords mentioning the destination), YouTube, Meta (location: people traveling to or interested in the area)
**Offer:** "The San Diego Relocation Guide": neighborhoods by vibe and commute (coastal, central, North County, East County, South Bay), cost of living, commute times to the bases and job hubs (Sorrento Valley, UTC, downtown), and a virtual-tour home search
**Hooks**
1. "PCSing to San Diego? Here's what your BAH can buy near North Island, Miramar or Pendleton."
2. "Moving to San Diego? Get the neighborhood guide locals wish they'd had."
3. "Tour homes from 1,000 miles away. I'll be your eyes on the ground."

**Google keywords:** "moving to san diego", "best neighborhoods in san diego", "VA loan realtor san diego", "homes near camp pendleton", "homes near miramar", "relocating to san diego"
**Creative:** Neighborhood tour videos (a 60-second walk-and-talk per area), FaceTime-tour testimonials
**KPI:** CPL ≤ $40; strong lead quality, as these buyers are usually serious and on a deadline

## Campaign 4: "Still Thinking About It?" (Retargeting) ⭐ Priority
**Goal:** Turn warm visitors into appointments
**Audiences:** Website visitors (180 days), video viewers (50%+), lead-form openers who didn't submit, Instagram/Facebook engagers
**Channels:** Meta, Google Display, YouTube
**Ads to rotate:**
- **Social proof:** five-star review cards ("Jan sold our home in 6 days, $12K over asking." Use only real reviews.)
- **Behind the scenes:** "Here's exactly how I market every listing" (photography, staging tips, launch plan)
- **Direct offer:** "Book a 15-minute call. No pressure, just a plan."
- **Market update:** monthly 60-second "San Diego market in a minute" video

**KPI:** Frequency 2–4 per week; cost per appointment ≤ $150

## Campaign 6: "Second Opinion" (Expired & FSBO Sellers)
**Goal:** Win frustrated sellers whose homes didn't sell
**Channels:** Direct mail + Meta lead ad + Google Search ("why didn't my house sell")
**Offer:** Free "Why It Didn't Sell" listing audit: photos, price, exposure, and a new launch plan
**Copy**
> Your home didn't sell. That's not your fault. It's a marketing problem.
> I'll give you a free, honest audit of what went wrong and exactly how I'd relaunch it.
> Jan `[LAST NAME]` · `[BROKERAGE NAME]` · DRE #`[NUMBER]`

**Note:** Market to expired listings only once the listing agreement has actually expired, and follow Do-Not-Call and TCPA rules for calls and texts.
**KPI:** Audit requests ≥ 5/month; 25%+ of audits become listings

## Campaign 7: "Know Someone?" (Sphere, Past Clients & Referrals)
**Goal:** Repeat business and referrals, the cheapest clients you will ever win
**Channels:** Meta custom audience (upload of your client list), email, SMS, YouTube
**Tactics**
- Quarterly "Your Home's Value Update" email to past clients
- Client appreciation event (pie pickup, shred day, summer movie night). Promote it with ads to the client list.
- Home anniversary video messages
- Referral thank-yous. **Check California DRE and RESPA rules** before offering anything of value for referrals; a handwritten note and public shout-out are always safe.

**Copy**
> The best compliment I can get is your referral. 💛
> If a friend, neighbor, or coworker is thinking about buying or selling, I'd love to help them the way I helped you.

**KPI:** 30%+ of annual business from repeat and referral clients
