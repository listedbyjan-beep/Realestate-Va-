# Campaign Strategy

## 1. Positioning
**Brand promise:** *"Listed by Jan. Sold with a plan."* A local, hands-on agent who markets every home like a launch, and a calm guide for buyers in a competitive market.

Proof points (fill in with real numbers):
- `[# HOMES SOLD]` homes sold, average `[X]`% of list price, `[X]` average days on market
- `[REVIEW COUNT]` five-star reviews
- Local roots in `[CITY/COUNTY]`, plus a full marketing package on every listing (pro photos, video, social launch)

## 2. Who we're targeting
| Segment | Their situation | Main worry | Our offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Move-up / move-down sellers** | Equity-rich homeowners who have outgrown their home or are downsizing | "What's my home worth, and can I time the buy and sell?" | Free home valuation plus a sell-then-buy plan |
| **First-time buyers** | Renters paying `$[X]`+/month | "Can I afford it? Is now the time?" | First-time buyer workshop or guide, plus a lender intro |
| **Relocation / military** (VA has a large military and federal workforce) | PCS orders, new job | "I need to buy remotely, fast" | Virtual tours, relocation guide, VA-loan know-how |
| **Expired / FSBO sellers** | Their listing didn't sell | "The last agent failed me" | A second-opinion marketing plan |
| **Investors** | Want cash flow or rehab properties | "Where are the deals?" | Weekly off-market and value-add list |
| **Past clients & sphere** | Already trust Jan | Nothing yet. Keep Jan top of mind | Home anniversary updates, referral rewards where legal |

> Targeting must follow housing ad rules. You **cannot** target by age, gender, ZIP code or familial status. Define these segments with *messaging and offers*, not demographic targeting. See `compliance-checklist.md`.

## 4. Channel mix and monthly budget
Example monthly budgets. Scale up whatever produces the lowest cost per *qualified* lead.

| Channel | Role | $1,500/mo | $3,000/mo | $6,000/mo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google Search (seller + buyer intent keywords) | High-intent leads | $600 | $1,000 | $1,800 |
| Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Housing category | Lead forms, video, retargeting | $600 | $1,100 | $2,000 |
| Retargeting (Meta + Google Display/YouTube) | Convert warm traffic | $300 | $400 | $700 |
| YouTube / CTV local | Brand awareness | n/a | $300 | $800 |
| Zillow/Realtor.com, or direct mail to listings | Supplemental | n/a | $200 | $700 |

Rule of thumb: about **60% on lead generation, 25% on retargeting, 15% on brand**.

## 5. KPIs and targets
| Metric | Starting target (adjust after 30 days) |
|---|---|
| Cost per lead, Meta lead form | $8–$25 buyer, $20–$60 seller |
| Cost per lead, Google Search | $30–$90 |
| Landing page conversion rate | 8%+ (valuation page), 15%+ (guide download) |
| Speed to lead | **Under 5 minutes** (the biggest lever you control) |
| Lead → appointment | 10–15% |
| Appointment → signed agreement | 30–50% |
| Cost per signed client | Track monthly against your average commission |

These benchmark ranges are typical, not guaranteed. Replace them with your own data after the first 30–60 days.

**Tracking setup:** Meta Pixel + Conversions API, Google Ads conversion tag, GA4, UTM tags on every ad, call tracking numbers per channel, and every lead tagged by campaign in the CRM.

## 6. 90-day rollout
| Weeks | Actions |
|---|---|
| **1–2: Build** | Brand assets, 3 landing pages (Valuation, Buyer Guide, Consult), tracking, CRM automations, compliance review, film 6–10 short videos |
| **3–4: Launch** | Campaign 1 (Seller Valuation), Campaign 2 (First-Time Buyer), Campaign 4 (Retargeting); Google Search live |
| **5–8: Optimize** | Pause ads with a CTR under 0.8% or CPL more than 2x target; test 3 new hooks per week; add Campaign 3 (Relocation) |
| **9–12: Scale** | Move budget to the top 2 campaigns, add Campaign 5 (Just Listed/Sold) and Campaign 6 (Expired/FSBO), launch Campaign 7 (Sphere/Referral) |

**Weekly testing cadence:** one variable at a time (hook → creative → offer → audience). Each ad gets about 1,000 impressions or $50 before you judge it.
