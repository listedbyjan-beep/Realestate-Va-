# Compliance Checklist (review before every launch)

This is a practical checklist, not legal advice. Confirm the details with your responsible broker.

## California DRE advertising rules
- [ ] **Your DRE license number** appears on all first-point-of-contact marketing: ads, social profiles, lead-form pages, videos, postcards, business cards (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §10140.6)
- [ ] **Your name** with the license number, and your **responsible broker's name** (or registered brokerage name) shown clearly; no ad that hides that you're a licensee
- [ ] Team names follow DRE team-name rules (a team name needs the responsible broker's identity next to it)
- [ ] Every statistic ("sold in 6 days," "$40K over asking") is real, recent and documented; no misleading claims

## Fair Housing (federal Fair Housing Act + California FEHA and Unruh Act)
- [ ] No wording that states or implies a preference based on a protected class. California covers more classes than federal law, including race, color, religion, sex, gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, marital status, national origin, ancestry, familial status, disability, source of income (including Section 8 vouchers), genetic information, and military or veteran status. Check the Civil Rights Department's current list.
- [ ] Describe the **property**, not the ideal buyer: "walk to the park," not "perfect for young families" or "ideal for singles"
- [ ] Equal Housing Opportunity logo or statement on landing pages and print

## Meta (Facebook/Instagram): Special Ad Category "Housing"
- [ ] Housing category selected on every real estate ad
- [ ] No age, gender, or ZIP-code targeting; location radius of at least 15 miles
- [ ] Lookalike audiences are replaced by Special Ad Audiences or broad targeting

## Google Ads: housing personalized advertising policy
- [ ] No targeting or exclusions by age, gender, parental status, marital status, or ZIP code

## Leads, privacy and messaging
- [ ] TCPA: get express written consent (a checkbox on the form) before automated texts or calls
- [ ] Check the Do-Not-Call list before cold calling expireds or FSBOs
- [ ] CAN-SPAM: unsubscribe link and physical address in every email
- [ ] California privacy: a privacy policy on every landing page that collects leads (check with your broker whether CCPA/CPRA applies to your business)
- [ ] Referral fees go only to licensees; check DRE and RESPA rules before any reward or lender co-marketing
- [ ] Written permission for every testimonial, client photo, and video
